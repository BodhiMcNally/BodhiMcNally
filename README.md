<h1>Hi 👋, I'm Bodhi</h1>

<p>
I'm a Doctor of Medicine student at the University of Sydney, based at Westmead Clinical School, with a Bachelor of Science in Data Science and Genetics & Genomics.
</p>

<p>
My background is in quantitative healthcare research and analytics. Prior to commencing medicine, I worked at AstraZeneca applying statistical and computational methods to real-world healthcare data across multiple therapeutic areas. I now teach undergraduate data science and statistics as a Casual Academic in the School of Mathematics and Statistics at the University of Sydney.
</p>

<p>
My developing research interests lie in academic surgery and urology, particularly urological oncology, robotic and minimally invasive surgery, surgical outcomes, and the application of clinical data to questions of treatment selection, perioperative outcomes and patient recovery. I am also a Surgical Research Scholar with RISE, where I am developing experience in collaborative surgical research and academic medicine.
</p>

<p>
Beyond research and teaching, I have held senior leadership and governance roles across major student organisations. I am particularly interested in strengthening pathways into research during medical school and the prevocational years, giving students and junior doctors opportunities to contribute meaningfully to academic work while developing the next generation of clinician-scientists.
</p>
